# Code of Conduct

Automated SDR is a small project built by and for people running small businesses. We want
everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests and discussions) to feel welcome.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not developers, and every question is a fair one.
- Assume good intent, give specific and constructive feedback, and accept it gracefully.
- Keep other people's data private. Never post someone's email address, leads or messages.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language, in public or in private.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Using this project, or asking for help with it, to send spam or to get around the law
  (CAN-SPAM, CASL, GDPR/PECR) or its built-in compliance features.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, code or issues that break these rules, and may block
people who keep breaking them. Report a problem privately through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/Fred-In-tech/automated-sdr-agent/security/advisories/new),
or by messaging [@Fred-In-tech](https://github.com/Fred-In-tech) on GitHub. Reports are kept
confidential.

This code is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
